# Archit & Anubha | Wedding Invitation

Interactive digital wedding invitation website inspired by the sacred city of Varanasi and the divine blessings of Lord Shiva.

- **Haldi, Vivah & Vidai**: Basdila Khurd, Post – Laxmipur Baboo, District – Kushinagar (UP)
- **Date**: 24 November 2026

## Family Details
- **Mishra Family** & **Tiwari Family**
- **Archit** (Son of Shri. Upendra Tiwari & Late. Shobha Devi)
- **Anubha** (Daughter of Shri. Prabhunath Mishra & Smt. Vimla Devi)

## Highlights
- Sacred royal crest & Varanasi golden-hour theme
- Frosted glassmorphism venue cards with Google Maps navigation
- Interactive celebrations schedule & animated photo moments
- Live wedding countdown timer
- Ambient wedding background music with play/mute & cinematic auto-scroll controls

## Getting Started
Open `index.html` in your browser or run a local server:
```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8000`.
